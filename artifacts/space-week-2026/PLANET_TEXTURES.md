# Planet texture sources

The Science Awareness scene lazily loads planetary surface textures from Solar System Scope's Solar Textures pack. Textures are available under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license (CC BY 4.0), which permits commercial and non-commercial use with attribution.

Source and attribution: Solar System Scope, “Solar Textures,” https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/ — textures based on NASA elevation and imagery data; used under CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.
